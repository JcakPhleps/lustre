Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Lustre (“Lustre”) is a chat application operated by Phelps Experimental Softwares (“P.E.S.,” “We,” “Us”), whose owner and operator is Jack D. Phelps (“He,” “Him”), based in Massachusetts, USA. This policy explains what We collect, how We use it, and your choices. Questions: help.lustre.pes@outlook.com.

1. Who can use Lustre

Lustre is for people 13 and older. During registration We ask for your date of birth to confirm you meet this age. If you indicate you are under 13, you cannot create an account at this time.

2. What We collect

  • Account: your username, a password (handled by Google Firebase Authentication — We never see or store your raw password), and an optional recovery email.
  • Date of birth: collected at registration to verify you meet the minimum age.
  • Profile: display name and optional profile picture, which are visible to other users.
  • Messages & media: the text, images, and voice messages you send. These are end-to-end encrypted (Section 5) — We store only the encrypted data and cannot read their content.
  • Metadata (not encrypted): who you message and when, your friends and friend requests, usernames, online/“active” status, typing and read-receipt indicators, and timestamps.
  • On your device: your app settings are stored in your browser, not on Our servers.
  • Technical/log data: Our host and Google Firebase may log technical data such as IP address and browser type to operate and secure the service.

3. How We use it

To create your account, deliver and sync your messages and calls, show presence and friends, maintain security, and respond to support. We do not sell your data, and We do not run advertising or analytics.

4. Who We share it with

We use service providers who process data on Our behalf: Google Firebase (authentication and database), a TURN relay provider (metered.ca) that relays encrypted call traffic when a direct connection fails, and GitHub Pages (website hosting). Your profile and the messages you send are also shared with the people you communicate with, and any signed-in user can find you by username to send a request. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. End-to-end encryption

Message content is encrypted on your device and decryptable only by you and the intended recipients, using keys We never hold. Calls are encrypted in transit by WebRTC. Encryption protects content, not metadata (Section 2). Because only you hold your key, if you forget your password and haven’t saved it, your past messages cannot be recovered by anyone, including Us.

6. Data retention & deletion

We keep your account and content until you delete them. When you delete your account, We remove your profile, friends, presence, and your one-on-one chats; your messages in group chats are anonymized to “[deleted user]” rather than deleted, since they are interwoven with other people’s messages.

7. Your choices and rights

You can view and edit your profile in the app, change your password, and delete your account. To make a request or ask a question, email help.lustre.pes@outlook.com. We honor access and deletion requests regardless of where you live.

8. Security

We use end-to-end encryption, encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and database access rules that restrict data to authorized users. No system is perfectly secure, and We cannot guarantee absolute security. As a Massachusetts-based operator, We maintain reasonable safeguards for personal information.

9. Children

Lustre is not directed to children under 13 and We do not knowingly collect their data. If We learn an account belongs to someone under 13, We will delete it.

10. International users

Lustre is operated from the United States and data is processed in the U.S. (via Google Firebase). If you use Lustre from outside the U.S., you consent to that processing.

11. Changes

We may update this policy; We will change the “Last updated” date and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.

12. Contact

Phelps Experimental Softwares — help.lustre.pes@outlook.com.

This policy is provided for transparency and is not legal advice.
